## Analyses for figure 5

Manual annotation of basalis glands on proliferative-phase Visium HD sections.

| Path | What it does | Panels |
|---|---|---|
| `TissueTag_manual_annotations/TissueTag_VisiumHD_<sample>_basalis_glands.ipynb` | Annotates myometrium, basalis, functionalis, lumen and basalis glands by hand in TissueTag on 16 µm Visium HD bins. Computes compartment distances and the basalis-to-lumen axis, treating glands as basalis. Plots a dot plot of basalis gland markers and the spatial expression of SLC6A2 and CYP24A1 | 5e, ED 5a |
